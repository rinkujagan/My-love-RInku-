A Letter For Rinku

Some feelings deserve their own little page.

My dearest Rinku, Kabhi-kabhi sochta hoon ki tum meri life mein aakar kitni saari ordinary cheezon ko special bana deti ho. Tumhari baatein, tumhari smile aur tumhara chhota sa attention bhi mere din ko beautiful bana sakta hai. Main tumse har din perfect hone ka promise nahi karta. Lekin main ye zaroor chahta hoon ki hum ek doosre ko samjhein, ek doosre ki baat sunein aur difficult days mein bhi ek doosre ka haath na chhodein. Tumhe hamesha khush rakhna meri wish hai, aur jab tum khush na ho, toh tumhare paas baithkar tumhari baat sunna bhi mere liye pyaar hai. Mujhe tumhare saath chhoti-chhoti khushiyan collect karni hain—random conversations, silly jokes, peaceful evenings aur woh moments jahan kuch khaas karna zaroori hi na ho. Thank you for being you, Rinku. Tum mere liye bahut special ho. ❤️
Forever with love, JAGAN ♡
